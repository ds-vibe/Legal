Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 20, 2025

Data Collection

Circuit Breaker collects minimal data necessary for leaderboard functionality:

  • Username: Optional name you choose when submitting a score.
  • Game Statistics: Score, level reached, max combo, survival time, and game mode (Normal/Hard/Zen)

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for:

  • Displaying leaderboard rankings
  • Allowing you to compete with other players

Data Storage

Data is stored in Supabase, a third-party database service.

Your Rights

You can choose to submit your score or not, using a real or fake name. Submitted data is publicly visible on the leaderboard as part of the game's competitive features.

Children's Privacy

Circuit Breaker does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

Support

Need help or have questions about Circuit Breaker?

Contact Us

For support, bug reports, or questions:

Email: circuitbreakergame@proton.me

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I submit a score? After a game ends, enter your name and tap "Submit Score to Leaderboard".
  • Can I delete my score? Currently, scores cannot be deleted within the app. Contact circuitbreakergame@proton.me.
  • Why don't I see my score? Only the top scores are displayed on the leaderboard. Keep playing to improve your rank!
