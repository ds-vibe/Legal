Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026

Data Collection

Our Games are designed to collect as little data as possible. The Games do not require an account, and we do not use advertising networks, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. The information we handle falls into the following categories:

  • Optional Leaderboard Data: If you choose to submit a score to a global leaderboard, we collect the name or initials you enter (which may be real or made up), your score, the game mode or difficulty, and related game statistics (such as level reached, combo, or survival time, depending on the Game). Submitting a score is always optional. Leaderboard data is sent to and stored by a third-party database service and is publicly visible to other players as part of the Games’ competitive features.
  • Locally Stored Data: The Games save certain information on your device only, such as local high scores, your last-used name or initials, selected difficulty or mode, settings, and tutorial progress. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.
  • Technical Data: When a Game communicates with our service providers (for example, to load or submit leaderboard scores) or when you play a web version of a Game, standard technical information such as your IP address, device or browser type, and request timestamps may be automatically recorded in server logs by those providers. We do not use this information to identify or track you.
  • Sharing Features: Some Games include a share button that passes your score, a short message, and/or an image of your results to your device’s native share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is handled entirely by the app you select. We do not receive or store shared content.

How We Use Your Data

Information we collect is used solely for:

  • Displaying leaderboard rankings
  • Allowing you to compete with other players
  • Operating, maintaining, and securing the Games
  • Responding to support requests

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Leaderboard data is stored in Supabase, a third-party database service. Web versions of the Games are hosted by Vercel, which maintains standard technical server logs. These providers process data on our behalf.

Leaderboard entries are retained until you ask us to delete them or until we reset or retire a leaderboard. Locally stored data remains on your device until you clear the Game’s data or uninstall it.

Your Rights and Choices

You may request deletion of a leaderboard entry at any time by contacting us at the email address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights regarding your personal information; please contact us to exercise them.

Children's Privacy

The Games do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it.
